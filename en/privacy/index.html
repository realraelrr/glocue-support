Privacy Policy

App: GloCue (练完啦)
Service Provider: Xurui Liang (梁栩瑞)
Revision: 1.0.1 (November 20, 2026); applies to GloCue 1.0.1 and later, starting on the public release of 1.0.1.

View the previous public text for GloCue 1.0

This policy is provided in Simplified Chinese, English, and Traditional Chinese. All three versions are intended to be consistent. If there is any inconsistency, the Simplified Chinese version controls to the extent permitted by applicable law.

GloCue is a local-first app for recording dance practice, linking practice videos, and reviewing your history. “Local-first” means the app uses an on-device replica for interaction; it does not mean that data stays only on the device. This policy explains how the local replica, Apple private cloud services, and Photos media work together.

1. Information We Handle

We do not require a developer account and do not operate our own server to receive your practice content. You do not need to sign in to an Apple Account to view, create, edit, or delete local records. iCloud Sync is optional. Regular sync uses private CloudKit when you enable sync and the verified Apple Account matches the local library’s binding. If you turn sync on again and explicitly agree to merge into a different account, the app first reads that account’s relevant data and checks media before updating the local binding and starting sync. The app handles:

  • Structured data: Profile name and image pointer, studios and dance cards, practice records, and related dates, times, prices, teachers, course types, songs, notes, difficulty, and media pointers.
  • Media information: photos or videos you explicitly choose, iCloud Photos identity references, and file descriptors for automatic/manual posters, and Profile images.
  • Device state: limited preferences and opaque CloudKit account-binding state used to prevent local replicas for different Apple Accounts from being mixed.

The app keeps an account binding on your device to keep Apple Account libraries separate. When sync is enabled, the library manifest in your private CloudKit database also stores an opaque CloudKit account identifier and uses its hash to form the manifest record name.

3. Photos Permission and Media Processing

The app requests access to Apple Photos when needed:

  • The system picker itself does not require full Photos access. Read access lets the app map authorized media to an identity that can resolve across devices using the same iCloud Photos library.
  • Add-only access is used when you choose to export a slowed practice video to your system photo library.
  • When mapping succeeds, the app stores only a Photos cloud reference and does not copy the source video. That reference is not an independent video backup; the target device still needs the same iCloud Photos library, suitable permission, completed sync/download, and the continuing existence of the source asset.
  • If the app cannot read the selected video or obtain its Photos cloud identifier, it leaves the video out. You can still save your practice record.
  • If your photos or videos are stored in iCloud Photos, iOS may download the original media from iCloud to complete playback, thumbnail generation, or export. That process is handled by Apple system services.

You can change or revoke Photos access at any time in iOS Settings. Some video links, poster generation, export, or playback features may stop working after permission is revoked.

4. Network and Third Parties

The app has no developer account system, advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, or developer server that receives practice content. When iCloud Sync is enabled, structured data and app-owned media sync through separate metadata and media zones in the same Apple private CloudKit database, while Photos-backed originals use iCloud Photos. These services are tied to your Apple Account and Apple system settings.

Network activity includes:

  • After you accept the agreement, the app may send one lightweight request to an Apple connectivity test URL so Apple/iOS network preparation can happen earlier. This is not a GloCue feature request, is not used to upload, sync, analyze, or fetch app content, and does not include your practice records, photos, videos, or notes.
  • Private CloudKit synchronizes/restores structured data and app-owned media; each media record contains one CKAsset within the 48 MB product ceiling. iCloud Photos resolves and downloads Photos-backed originals. Sync converges according to Apple’s system schedule and is not guaranteed to be immediate.
  • If you have opted in to share Apple App Analytics with developers in your device settings, Apple may provide summary or diagnostic information that usually does not directly identify you. We do not integrate our own or third-party behavioral tracking.

5. Storage, Deletion, and Backups

The device stores a local SwiftData replica and on-demand media copies. When iCloud Sync is enabled, business metadata is stored in a private CloudKit metadata zone, and automatic/manual posters and Profile JPEG masters are stored as CKAssets in a separate private CloudKit media zone. For Photos-backed videos, the app stores only references; original videos remain managed by iCloud Photos. Thumbnails are bounded cache entries.

Uninstalling removes app-local copies, but does not automatically delete private CloudKit data, media records, or originals in Photos from your Apple Account. A reinstall or another device using the same Apple Account with sync enabled may restore structured data and app-owned media after Apple services finish syncing/downloading.

Deleting a record removes it from visible content, but the current version retains its field contents, save sources, and deletion marker in local sync information and, when sync is enabled, in your private CloudKit database. This supports merging edits from offline devices; a later user save may restore the record. Deletion does not immediately erase this retained sync information. Automatic poster generation does not restore deleted records.

In the current version, removing or replacing a record, video, poster, or Profile-image pointer does not automatically delete immutable CloudKit media records. Pointer deletion and cloud-record deletion are separate operations, so orphan records may continue using your iCloud storage. The app does not infer safe cloud deletion from a single device’s reference scan or a grace period.

Local data remains visible and editable if you are not signed in to an Apple Account, turn off iCloud Sync, or iCloud becomes temporarily unavailable. Temporary account unavailability pauses sync until the account becomes available again. A confirmed different Apple Account stops sync and prevents the old local library from being mixed with the new account. The app does not automatically delete, migrate, or rebind local data, and this does not delete cloud data belonging to the prior Apple Account. When you turn sync on again, you may explicitly agree to merge current local content with the new account’s data. Preparation failure or cancellation before commit preserves the previous binding and local content. Transfer does not modify or delete cloud data in the prior account. Unavailable required media prevents transfer. Photos videos retain only references accessible on this device; transfer does not copy Photos originals. Playback on other devices depends on their Photos access and the availability of the originals. A Photos reference inaccessible on this device prevents transfer.

After you explicitly confirm an account transfer, the app reads the new account’s data and may prepare its private library using that account’s own cloud content or an empty library. This preparation can occur before your device changes its binding. Records and JPEGs being transferred from the old account can upload only after the device commits the new binding. If preparation fails or you cancel before that commit, the old binding and old account’s cloud data remain unchanged. The new account may retain some or all of its prepared library, but receives none of the content being transferred from the old account.

If GloCue appears in Settings, you can delete its iCloud data under Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Storage (or Manage Account Storage) → GloCue. This is separate from device backups and does not delete local practice records or original Photos media. Once the app confirms that the cloud library was erased, it stops syncing and does not rebuild automatically. If you later turn sync on and confirm rebuilding, the app rebuilds from this device’s current local records and related app images.

6. Your Choices and Local-First Limits

You can view, correct, or delete records in the app; turn iCloud Sync on or off in Data & Privacy; export a password-encrypted .glocue backup and choose content to import; revoke Photos permission in iOS Settings; or uninstall to remove local data. The backup includes the complete current local business records and required App-owned media, but excludes hidden sync information for deleted records. Photos videos include references, not originals. A backup is not a live cloud snapshot. Keep your password safe; the app does not store it and cannot recover it. We cannot remotely view or operate your private CloudKit content from a developer server. Photos, iCloud Photos, private CloudKit, Apple App Analytics, and exported files are managed by Apple or by you.

7. Security Measures

We reduce data exposure through a local replica, Apple private databases, account-identity isolation, and minimal Photos access. The app does not operate a server that receives practice data and does not integrate advertising or third-party behavioral tracking SDKs. Security also depends on your device lock, system updates, Apple Account security, and permissions you grant.

8. Sharing

Except for Apple system services involved in App Store distribution, iCloud, Photos, crash diagnostics, or system permissions, and email provider and Outlook/Microsoft processing when you contact us by email, we do not sell, rent, or share your personal content with third parties. If we add a service that changes these practices, we will update this policy and obtain consent where required.

9. Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed to children and does not encourage minors to use it without guardian supervision. Users below the digital age of consent in their location, or users under 14 in Mainland China, should use the app only if a parent or guardian decides to allow it and manages the related data and permissions. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through our own server. If you believe a child has used the app or sent us personal information without appropriate guardian consent, please contact us.
