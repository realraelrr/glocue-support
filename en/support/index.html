I selected a video, but it won’t attach

In Record, open the class, tap the pencil, then tap + in the video section. Select the clip from Photos. Once it appears in the editor, tap Save. If the clip is in Files or a chat, save it to Photos first. You can save a record without a video.

First time, or previously denied access. You can grant access to just the clips you need. If you see “No access to Photos.”, tap Open Settings. Change GloCue’s Photos access there, return to the editor, and tap + or use Choose Again on the missing clip. If the device restricts Photos access, ask the person who manages it. You can still save your other changes.

The picker shows the clip, but GloCue says “No access to this video.” Tap Manage Photos Access and add that exact clip to the allowed selection. Back in the editor, tap Save if the clip has appeared. If it’s still attaching, wait. If it never appears, tap + to add it or Choose Again on the missing thumbnail to replace it, then save. If it’s already attached, look through this record’s thumbnails.

The Save button is spinning. Stay in the editor. Finish any open permission prompt. If attaching takes longer, tap the clock in place of the + tile, or next to the video heading when replacing. Choose Keep Waiting or Cancel. After the attachment finishes or is canceled, save your other edits.

An exclamation mark appears. Tap it and tap Retry Photos Connection if shown, or choose the clip again. A failed replacement keeps the previous link.

A video I added won’t play

On the device with the problem, open Photos and try playing the same clip. A visible cover in GloCue doesn’t mean the original is available there.

  • Photos can play it. Check GloCue’s Photos permission. If access is limited, use Manage Photos Access when shown to add the specific clip. If you see Open Settings, adjust access there. If Photos hasn’t asked for access yet and the player offers Retry, tap it to request access. If Photos access is restricted by the device, check with its administrator.
  • Photos is downloading the original, or GloCue reports a temporary failure. Connect to a working network and wait until Photos can play it, then retry in GloCue if the button is shown. You can also reopen the player. Keep the original link while checking.
  • “This video is temporarily unavailable in Photos. Try again later.” Check that Photos still has the original on this device, then return to the player and tap Retry later. If Photos can’t find it either, check the next two cases.
  • The original is missing on this device. Check Photos on the source device. If you use iCloud Photos, make sure the receiving device has the same Photos library, the clip is available, and GloCue has access to it. GloCue’s iCloud sync handles class records, not Photos originals.
  • You confirmed it was deleted. Look in Photos → Recently Deleted first. If you can recover it there, try playing it in GloCue again. Deleted clips can usually be recovered within 30 days. With iCloud Photos, deleting a clip also deletes it from other devices using that library. Apple’s Photos recovery guide.

Only another copy is available, or the wrong clip was linked. Save the correct clip to Photos. In the record editor, tap Choose Again on the missing clip, select the replacement and save. If the button in Practice opens the record first, tap the pencil to reach the editor. To replace a working clip, add the correct one with +, then tap × on the old thumbnail, choose Delete and tap Save. Removing the link leaves the original in Photos.

Find a check-in or add a missed class

Right after check-in. Press and hold the record for about a second until the progress finishes, then release. The basic entry is saved. Save the glow opens that same record so you can add a teacher, song, notes, or video. The × on the record cancels and deletes that pending check-in.

Find an entry. In Record, pick the practice date or search for details you entered. The default list shows the latest date with a record. Check the previous date if you checked in shortly after midnight: the entry is filed by its start date. For example, a check-in at 00:20 defaults to 23:15 the night before through 00:15. Look under Status → Needs tags/video for entries without details.

First check-in. Tap Later during album setup if you like. To change your name or album image later, tap the pencil by your name in Check-in.

Add a missed class. If you’re searching, tap Cancel to return to the calendar. If the date is already displayed, go straight to the button. Otherwise select today or an earlier date and tap Backfill check-in below that day’s entries. Set a start or end time; the app fills in the other end one hour away. Adjust as needed, then save. An unsaved draft creates no entry. If you see Time Overlap, check the existing entry. Use Save Anyway only if you intend to keep both.

The record is still loading after saving. If you’re on the record details screen (not in the editor), use Retry if shown, or return to the date in Record and check whether the entry is already there before adding another. For a spinner in the editor, see saving while a video attaches.

My records are missing on a new device

  1. If you still have the old device, confirm it can open the records. On both devices verify the Apple Account, then open Record → More (…) → Data & Privacy. Set up iCloud Sync on the old device first if it holds the complete records. If this is the first setup, let it finish creating the library there before opening sync on the new device. You can also export a backup first.
  2. Enable iCloud Sync on the new device using the same account. If the old device is gone, this works for records already synced to iCloud. If you have an older backup, you can preview and import it. Use Check Again only if it appears. If the app reports insufficient iCloud storage, check the account’s cloud storage.
  3. Open some older records on the new device and check notes and covers. Check video originals separately. Keep the source device until you verify the result.

Asked to rebuild the iCloud library? On a device with the complete records, first-time setup for an account without a GloCue cloud library can show Rebuild and Upload. It uploads the records and linked images on that device; it does not look for lost data. If an empty receiving device asks to rebuild, cancel and check the source device or backup. If you can’t identify a copy with your records, contact support before continuing.

Asked to transfer records or copy an older library? Transfer Records merges this device’s records into the current iCloud account. If you meant to use the original account, cancel and check the account first. If the transfer reports it can’t access Photos, restore access to the originals on the device with your records, then enable iCloud Sync there again. Copy and Join copies visible records and linked images from the old library into the new one. Later changes made in the older app won’t follow. If you’re unsure which library to use, contact support first.

Back up, import, or recover records

Export. Go to Record → More (…) → Data & Privacy → Export My Data. Enter and confirm a password of at least eight characters. Tap the up-arrow export icon and save the .glocue file in Files. Keep the password and a copy of the file you can access later. The backup includes records, dance cards, and the app’s required images. It includes links to Photos videos, not the original clips.

Preview or restore. Choose Import My Data, select the .glocue file and enter the original password. Preview makes no changes; you can cancel from it. The video count includes Photos references. Records absent from the backup are kept.

Restore a deleted item, or enable the Import button. Open a backup made before the deletion. Check new items. For changed or locally deleted items, choose This Device or Backup. Choose Backup for the item you want back. Review related records, cards, studios and videos when prompted. If an item’s identity is incompatible, choose This Device. If local changes refresh the preview, review your choices again. Tap Import after reviewing the full preview. Imported changes can sync to other devices if iCloud Sync is on.

File or password problem. If the file is unavailable, download it in Files before choosing it again. If the app reports low device storage, free local space. Check the original password and an intact copy of the file. An old JSON report can’t be imported. A forgotten password can’t be recovered. If you still have a device with the records, export again with a new password.

A sync conflict is blocking export. In Data & Privacy → Review Sync Conflicts, compare the two versions before selecting This Device Data or Other Device Data. Your choice applies immediately, usually to the listed fields only. Choosing Deleted removes the item. If you see Accept Deletion and Restore This Device’s Version, the first removes the local item and the second sends your edited version to iCloud. Then try exporting again. If a linked record or image is missing, check the record and sync status and retry once it’s available.

Before deleting or uninstalling. Deleting a record leaves its Photos originals but can sync the record deletion. Turning off iCloud Sync keeps local records. Deleting the app removes its local copy. Keep the device and app that still show your records until you verify the transfer.

Find a video in Practice or save it at a different speed

Practice lists records with linked videos. Clear the filters first, and in Record check whether the video was attached and saved. Tap the thumbnail to start practicing. For a multi-video entry, tap the preview with the clip count, then tap the clip you want.

In the Practice player, adjust speed or toggle the whole-video loop. To save a copy, choose a speed other than 1× and tap the down-arrow button at the top right (Save video at selected speed). Allow GloCue to add to Photos if asked. Stay on that clip until it finishes, then find the new copy in Photos. If the app says it needs permission to add to Photos, adjust the permission in Settings and save again. If the original won’t play, check access to it first.

Why don’t my dance-card visits or practice stats match?

Open the record, tap the pencil, tap Change next to the card, pick a card and save. You can also choose Add card, then return to the record and save. If a card isn’t available, check its validity on the practice date, and whether the total or period limit is used up. In the card’s progress view, check the unit: two classes in one day can count as two uses but only one check-in day. A time-limited card may show days elapsed instead.

In Analysis, check the selected date range and each record’s duration. A backfilled record with neither start nor end entered can count as 60 minutes. Edit the record to correct it.

Share a recap. In Analysis, select a date range with practice data and tap the share icon at the top right. Under Share content, deselect cards marked Includes names if you don’t want teacher or song names shown. Keep at least one card selected. Swipe through the previews, then tap the share icon to send or save the image.

Contact support

Email realrael.dev@outlook.com . Include your app version, device, system version, the step you were on, and the exact message. For video issues, say whether the same clip plays in Photos on that device. Screenshots are optional; hide private details. Please don’t send passwords, verification codes, backups, or private videos. We can’t view or restore your private iCloud data.
