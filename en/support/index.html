GloCue Support

GloCue is a dance practice journal for class notes, linked practice videos, and dance cards. It supports iPhone with iOS 17 or later and iPad with iPadOS 17 or later.

Contact

Email: realrael.dev@outlook.com

Please include your app version and build number, device model, OS version, steps to reproduce the issue, what you expected, and what happened. If helpful, attach screenshots with personal details obscured. Do not send Apple Account passwords, verification codes, payment information, or unnecessary private videos. We cannot view or restore your private iCloud content from a developer server.

We do not currently promise a fixed response time. Support language and communication arrangements depend on the individual exchange.

How do I record a practice?

Press and hold on the check-in screen to create today's entry, then fill in the teacher, style, song, class hours, notes, and chosen videos. Browse or edit existing entries, and find them through the calendar and search.

Can I use GloCue without iCloud?

Yes. Practice records are available locally, and unavailable iCloud service does not prevent you from viewing or editing local records. Choose whether to enable iCloud sync in Data & Privacy. Cross-device sync needs the same Apple Account and available Apple services. Sync is not a promise of immediate completion.

Why is a video unavailable on another device?

A linked Photos video remains managed by Photos. The other device needs the relevant Photos permission, the same iCloud Photos library, and time for the original to sync or download. Check that the original opens in Apple Photos and that network and storage are available. A linked video is not an independent backup; deleting the original or changing access may make it unavailable.

What can I check when iCloud sync is unavailable?

Check the sync setting in Data & Privacy, the Apple Account, the network, and available iCloud storage. If accounts do not match, GloCue preserves local content and pauses sync; it does not automatically transfer the old account's data to a new account. Do not begin troubleshooting by uninstalling the app or deleting iCloud data. Contact support if you still need help.

How do I export or delete data?

Use the export option in Data & Privacy to produce a JSON report of the complete records available locally. It contains no media files and is not an importable backup. You can delete entries in the app. Removing a video link or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete all cloud media. See the privacy policy for retention and deletion details.

How do I change Photos access?

Find GloCue's Photos permission in system Settings and adjust it as needed. Revoking access can affect playback and processing of linked media.
